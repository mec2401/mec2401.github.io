01HCI · Individual project

Clearer checkout choices

An order and price assistant exploring how a bakery kiosk could help customers understand pack sizes, upgrades, and value.

Read project notes

The question

When someone changes an order, how can the interface make the pricing consequences easier to understand?

The exploration

I explored menu prompts, smart labels such as “Best value,” and an order-review screen. A five-to-six pack upgrade was one of the scenarios used to examine how customers compare options.

The process

The coursework included survey and interview needfinding, a heuristic evaluation, alternative prototypes, and evaluation planning.

Scope: an academic design exploration, rather than a launched retail product.

02HCI · Team project

Plant care

A team exploration of how people track plant growth, watering, and light—and how useful information can become a clear next action.

Read project notes

The question

How can plant-care tools help people understand what their plants need without making care feel complicated?

The exploration

The team considered plant-stage identification, watering and light information, care history, weather context, and actionable tasks.

The process

Our needfinding plan combined surveys, interviews, and an apprenticeship approach. The work also considered outdoor gardens alongside indoor plants.

Scope: a collaborative academic project; the description reflects the team’s shared exploration.

03Statistics · Research

Collaboration patterns

Research into how faculty rank, university location, and research interests relate to collaboration.

Read project notes

The question

Which characteristics might help explain how faculty members collaborate?

The work

During my internship with Texas A&M’s Department of Statistics, I collected research publication and course information from more than 100 tenure-track faculty.

The methods

I used R for network, clustering, and regression analyses to investigate collaboration patterns.

Scope: undergraduate research experience, June–August 2022.
